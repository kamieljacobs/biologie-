# Bronnen hagedis, houding en tenen (28 okt 2026, zelf geopend)
Houding:
- Reilly & Delancey 1997, J. Exp. Biol. 200: 753 ('Sprawling locomotion in the lizard Sceloporus clarkii'): 'The foot clearly remains lateral to the knee'; crus and foot 'extended laterally from the knee joint'; 'a sprawling movement of the femur'.
- 'Foul and Loathsome' (eCampusOntario Pressbooks), hagedissen: 'retain the sprawling posture of early land tetrapods'; elleboog en knie buigen ± 90°; bovenste segmenten 'parallel to the ground', onderste verticaal.
- Encyclopedia.com, Lacertidae: 'strong legs, especially the larger back pair'.
Tenen:
- Encyclopaedia Britannica 1911, 'Reptiles' (Wikisource): 'Most well-footed reptiles retain all the five toes'; 'Claws ... are fully developed in all limbed reptiles'; falangen van hagedissen 2-3-4-5-3.
- Eyes on the Wild (blog): 'Lizards have five toes on all their feet.'
- Li e.a. 2013 (arXiv 1303.7292, zebrastaarthagedis): 'the longest fourth toe alone accounted for 63% of the hind foot length'.
- Williston, 'The Osteology of the Reptiles' h. 5: vijf vingers is de oorspronkelijke toestand, moderne hagedissen houden die.
