- Animal Diversity Web, Caretta caretta (https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Caretta_caretta), niveau A (Univ. Michigan): heart-shaped carapace, reddish-brown with olive tones; five pairs of pleural scutes, first pair touching the nuchal scute; huge heads; lays 110–130 round eggs; temperature-dependent sex determination.
