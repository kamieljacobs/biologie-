- Life (Sadava e.a., 11e druk) fig. 31.26 (https://digfir-published.macmillanusa.com/life11e/asset/img_ch31/life11e-fig-31-26-0.html), niveau A: head, thorax, abdomen; thorax bears three pairs of legs and in most groups two pairs of wings; no appendages on abdominal segments.
- NCSU Entomology 'Locomotion' (https://genent.cals.ncsu.edu/bug-bytes/thorax/locomotion), niveau A: three pairs of legs laterally in a wide stance; tripod = front and hind leg one side + middle leg other side.
- Zollikofer 1994, J. Exp. Biol. 192:95 (https://cob.silverchair.com/jeb/article-pdf/192/1/95/2854548/jexbio_192_1_95.pdf), niveau A: alternating tripod gait L1R2L3 ↔ R1L2R3 over a wide range of speeds.
- Hepburn & Radloff 2004, S. Afr. J. Sci. 100 (https://core.ac.uk/download/145032059.pdf), niveau A: hamuli on the hindwing couple it to the forewing into one aerofoil.
- PIAkey glossary (USDA/idtools, https://idtools.org/id/ants/pia/Glossary.html), niveau A: scape, petiole (waist segment), mesosoma (legs attached), gaster.
- DELTA British butterflies, Inachis io (https://www.delta-intkey.com/britin/pap/www/inachis.htm), niveau B: wingspan 63–75 mm; upperside purple with eyespots; underside very dark brownish-grey strigulated black; only 4 fully developed legs.

## Variant C
