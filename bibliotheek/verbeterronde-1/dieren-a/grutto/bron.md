## Variant C (snavel)
- Cornell All About Birds (niveau A): 'long bicolored bill'.
- Wikipedia, Bar-tailed godwit (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-tailed_godwit), niveau B: bill 'pink at the base and black towards the tip'.
  → snavel tweekleurig, roze basis, zwarte punt. Poten in vlucht: Kent Wildlife Trust 'feet only just stick out past their tail', HKBWS 'project moderately': bronnen oneens, poten niet aangepast.
