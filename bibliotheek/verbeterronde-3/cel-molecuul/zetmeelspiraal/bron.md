# Bronnen amylopectine (zelf geopend, 28 okt 2026)

- **ChEBI CHEBI:28057 amylopectin** (EMBL-EBI, niveau A) — https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/CHEBI:28057 : 'The chains are joined together by α(1→6) glycosidic linkages'; 'The branches in amylopectin typically contain 24 to 30 glucose residues.'
- **LibreTexts Chemistry, Fundamentals of GOB Chemistry 20.7** (open leerboek, niveau A) — https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Introductory_Chemistry/Fundamentals_of_General_Organic_and_Biological_Chemistry_(LibreTexts)/20%3A_Carbohydrates/20.07%3A_Some_Important_Polysaccharides_Based_on_Glucose : amylopectine 'linked primarily by α-1,4-glycosidic bonds but with occasional α-1,6-glycosidic bonds', 'branch points occurring about every 25–30 units'; amylose 'coiled like a spring, with six glucose monomers per turn'; glycogeen '8–12 glucose units between branches'.
- (Al in ronde 2 door de vorige maker geopend, niet opnieuw: Pesek & Silaghi-Dumitrescu 2024, Molecules 29:641.)
