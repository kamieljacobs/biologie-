Feel that urge to breathe again? That isn't your lungs running out of oxygen. It's your brain noticing something else entirely: carbon dioxide.

Every cell in your body is breaking down glucose right now, and one of the waste products is carbon dioxide. It dissolves in your blood and makes it slightly more acidic. Sensors in your brainstem pick up that change within seconds.
